# CoSphere — Help & User Guides

User guides and administrative documentation for CoSphere housing and community management.

---

## 1. Resident & Gate Access Setup

### 1.1 Generating Gate Passes
1. Open **CoSphere**.
2. Tap **Gate Security > New Guest Pass**.
3. Set visitor arrival time and expiration.
4. Share the generated QR / RFID clearance pass with your guest.

### 1.2 Maintenance Tickets
1. Go to **Services > Dispatch Ticket**.
2. Describe the maintenance issue and attach optional photo evidence.
3. Track real-time technician arrival and ticket resolution.

---

## 2. Administrator & Staff Roster

### 2.1 Managing Roster & Roles
Property managers can assign administrative roles (Admin, Security Staff, Resident) with instant sync across local ObjectBox databases.
