# Privacy Policy for Music Bridge

**Effective Date:** 4th August 2026  
**Developer / Operating Entity:** Cranbear ("Company," "we," "our," or "us")

---

## 1. Fundamental Privacy Principle: Zero Automatic Data Collection

**Music Bridge** ("the App") is architected under a strict **Local-First and Zero-Tracking Policy**. 

Unlike standard mobile applications, **Music Bridge does not automatically collect, harvest, log, track, or transmit any data**—personal, telemetry, analytics, or behavioral—by default. 

Simply installing, opening, configuring, or using the App does **not** grant us access to your personal information, listening habits, device identifiers, or usage metrics. We operate no background analytics trackers, no third-party telemetry SDKs, and no remote surveillance tools. All app operations execute locally on your physical device.

### 1.1 Application Runtime & Framework Telemetry
The compiled Application binary contains **zero runtime framework tracking, zero analytics telemetry, and zero automated phone-home reporting mechanisms**. Your app usage remains completely private and isolated to your personal device hardware.

---

## 2. On-Device Data Storage & Credential Isolation

To deliver seamless music migration, playlist synchronization, and speaker management, Music Bridge processes data strictly on your local hardware:

* **Hardware Keychain Isolation:** Authentication tokens, OAuth access credentials, and session keys for third-party streaming services (including Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, YouTube Music, and Tidal) are saved exclusively within your device's hardware-backed secure storage (Apple Keychain or Android Keystore). **At no point do your access tokens, passwords, or credentials touch Cranbear servers or third-party networks.**
* **Local Database Storage:** Your playlist metadata, track mappings, sync rules, and app preferences are stored locally in an **isolated, high-performance embedded application database (ObjectBox)** on your device sandbox.
* **No Remote Copying:** We do not maintain, upload, or sync central copies of your music libraries, playlists, or streaming histories to any cloud infrastructure operated by Cranbear.

---

## 3. Explicit, User-Initiated Data Transmissions Only

Data is transmitted to Cranbear **ONLY AND EXCLUSIVELY** when you voluntarily choose to ask for help, report a problem, or request a feature. 

We collect information **only** under the following three user-initiated scenarios:

1. **Setup & Configuration Assistance:** When you request technical help establishing pairing or network connections between your mobile device and connected audio speakers or streaming endpoints.
2. **Bug & Error Reporting:** When you encounter an unexpected software failure and choose to submit an error report for engineering investigation.
3. **Feature Requests & Inquiries:** When you submit a request for new service integrations or app enhancements.

### 3.1 Your Right to Download and Review Data Before Sending
We believe you should always be in complete control of your privacy. Before any support request, setup inquiry, or bug report is sent to us:

* **Optional Download & Inspection:** If you feel like reviewing what is inside the diagnostic package, Music Bridge gives you the built-in option to download and inspect the exact file package directly on your phone first.
* **Complete Transparency:** You can open, read, and verify every line of text, app log, or network configuration metadata before deciding whether or not to submit it.
* **No Automatic Submissions:** No data will ever leave your device until you give explicit consent after reviewing the information.

---

## 4. Scope & Limitations of Diagnostic Packages

When you choose to submit a support request, bug report, or feature inquiry, Music Bridge generates a localized diagnostic package. To protect your broader digital privacy, the contents of this package are strictly bounded as follows:

### What Is INCLUDED in the Diagnostic Package:
* **Music Bridge Application Logs:** System event logs generated **strictly by the Music Bridge application** during runtime to assist engineers in diagnosing app errors.
* **Relevant Local Network Metadata:** Technical configuration data **strictly limited** to the local Wi-Fi / IP connectivity status between your mobile device and connected smart speakers or audio playback hardware.
* **Local Music Bridge Database Snapshot:** A snapshot of the internal Music Bridge application database state, required for our engineering team to reproduce, debug, and resolve the reported issue.

### What Is EXCLUSIVELY EXCLUDED:
* **NO Operating System Logs:** We **do not** capture, read, or export general Android or iOS operating system logs, kernel outputs, or device-wide system event files.
* **NO Third-Party App Data:** We **do not** collect logs, data, or activity from any other application installed on your device.
* **NO Personal Identity or Contact Logs:** We **do not** extract your phone contacts, text messages, location history, call records, or media files.

---

## 5. Mandatory Affirmative Consent & Pre-Submission Download Rights

We believe you should have 100% visibility and legal authority over any data submitted to us.

* **Mandatory Affirmative Consent:** Before any diagnostic package or support payload can be transmitted to Cranbear, you must review a clear summary of the data payload and affirmatively check a consent agreement box. No automatic background transmissions will ever occur.
* **Right to Pre-Submission Inspection:** Prior to approving a transmission, Music Bridge provides a built-in **Download Diagnostic Package** feature. This empowers you to download, save, open, and inspect the raw text logs and database files on your device so you can independently verify every byte of data before choosing to send it to us.

---

## 6. Purpose Limitation, Retention & Non-Disclosure

Any diagnostic data voluntarily submitted by you is handled under strict legal data minimization principles:

* **Strict Purpose Limitation:** Submitted diagnostic packages are used solely and exclusively by Cranbear software engineers to fulfill your specific support request, resolve reported software bugs, or evaluate requested features.
* **Zero Commercialization or Sale:** We **never sell, rent, monetize, trade, or share** your diagnostic packages, support logs, or email communications with third-party advertisers, data brokers, or commercial marketing entities under any circumstances.
* **Data Retention & Destruction Schedule:** Support tickets and diagnostic payloads are retained only for as long as necessary to resolve the inquiry (not to exceed 90 days), after which they are permanently purged from our encrypted support infrastructure.

## 8. Global Legal Rights (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA & International Privacy Laws)

Regardless of your geographic location, Cranbear extends the following enforceable rights to all users:

* **Right to Refuse Data Sharing:** You are under no obligation to submit support logs or diagnostics. The App remains fully functional without sharing diagnostic data.
* **Right to Inspect Local Data:** You can view, inspect, and export all locally stored application database states directly from the App at any time.
* **Right to Erasure (Right to Be Forgotten):** You may request the permanent deletion of any support email or diagnostic ticket previously sent to us by contacting our privacy compliance team.
* **California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA):** Cranbear does not "sell" or "share" personal information as defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act.
