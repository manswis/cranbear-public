# Universal Scanner — User Guide & Help Center

Welcome to the **Universal Scanner** Help Center. Here you will find quick start guides, feature documentation, and troubleshooting steps for getting the most out of your scanner.

---

## 1. Quick Start Guide

### 1.1 Scanning Barcodes & QR Codes
1. Launch **Universal Scanner** on your device.
2. Align the camera viewfinder over any 1D barcode or 2D QR code.
3. The app automatically decodes the code on-device using local computer vision machine learning.
4. Tap the result card to open links, copy text, or share data.

### 1.2 Scanning Images from Gallery
1. Tap the **Gallery** icon on the top right of the scan screen.
2. Select an image containing a QR code or barcode.
3. Universal Scanner will decode the image instantly without uploading it to any server.

---

## 2. Advanced Features

### 2.1 Generating Custom QR Codes
* Go to the **Create** tab.
* Select your code type (URL, Wi-Fi Network, Contact vCard, Plain Text, or E-mail).
* Input your payload and tap **Generate Code**.
* Save as PNG/SVG or share directly.

### 2.2 Exporting Scan History
* Open **History**.
* Filter by date, favorite status, or format.
* Tap **Export** to save your scan database in **CSV**, **PDF**, or **JSON** format.

---

## 3. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: Does Universal Scanner send my scan data to the cloud?
**No.** All machine learning model execution and barcode decoding happen 100% locally on your device's processor.

### Q: Why does the app need Camera permission?
The camera stream is required strictly to detect barcodes in real-time. Frames are processed locally and discarded immediately.
